Arthas Menethil is the crown prince of Lordaeron in the *Warcraft* saga, and later the Lich King on the Frozen Throne. The story begins with a paladin prince desperate to shield his people: as plague closes in, he chooses harsher cuts to break the chain of infection; hunting a weapon that can kill what he fears, he sails to Northrend and takes up the runeblade Frostmourne. The sword wins him victories and removes the last room in which he could turn back.

The arc that follows turns on one pressure: when “decide now” overtakes every rite and every bond, what does a leader become. He kills his mentor, returns to unmake his homeland, and finally dons the Helm of Domination, stretching a single will into absolute command of the Scourge. The Lich King is not a sudden costume change; it is the cold remainder after a logic of salvation is pushed to its end.

In *Wrath of the Lich King*, Icecrown Citadel closes the line as a playable finale: frost, fire, and the throne are the stage of his power and the peak where players meet the story together. Much of Arthas’s lasting pull comes from that unbroken fall—each step still feels like doing the necessary thing, until “necessary” itself can no longer be told apart from ruin.
