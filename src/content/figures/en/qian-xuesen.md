Qian Xuesen had formidable theoretical depth, yet the question that held him was how complex systems are organized. Rockets and spaceflight are not solved by equations alone; aerodynamics, structure, materials, control, electronics, computation, manufacturing, and management must become one runnable whole.

He treated operations research and related methods as mathematical tools for systems engineering, including industrial and managerial use—the type who is strong at math and uses it to organize the real world.
