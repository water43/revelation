Taylor came out of machine shops and the steel industry. His questions were practical: how much can a worker do in a day, how many seconds does a motion take, which steps waste time, can the process be redesigned and standardized? He turned production into measurable parts and helped found scientific management.

He could time work on the floor and also abstract engineering intuition into *The Principles of Scientific Management*—closer to today’s industrial engineer, technical manager, and process optimizer than to a pure theorist.
