Hegel carried post-Kantian Idealism into a vast system: reality is not a static heap of things, but Spirit (*Geist*) unfolding dialectically in history. Opposites do not merely coexist; they are taken up into higher unities through sublation (*Aufhebung*).

The *Phenomenology of Spirit* traces consciousness from sense-certainty toward absolute knowing; the *Science of Logic* seeks the inner structure of thought and being. His accounts of right, ethical life, and the state place individual freedom within the development of ethical community.

However contested his system may be, Hegel’s treatments of history, alienation, recognition, and dialectic still shape Marxism, existentialism, and contemporary social theory.
