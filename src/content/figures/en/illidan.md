Illidan Stormrage is the night elf who takes the extreme road: where his brother Malfurion holds druidic balance, Illidan chooses the speed and the price of hunting demons. He drinks of the Well, takes fel, and trades his eyes for another kind of sight, remaking himself into a weapon that can look the Legion in the face. Others see broken rite and a desecrated body; he holds that unless someone becomes what can kill demons, the world burns first.

In *The Burning Crusade*, Outland and the Black Temple set him as the expansion’s face—ally and adversary at once, aims often aligned with the heroes, methods that leave no clean side to stand on. Prison, exile, and return are not a simple whitewash; they repeat one obsession: cut a greater curse with a cursed tool.

*Legion* brings him back to center stage. The story asks again whether sacrifice can be measured: who may remake a person into a monster, and who may keep calling him one after the victory. Illidan’s pull is that he rarely excuses himself—he admits he is terrible, and still treats that terror as necessary cost. The arc rests on the needed heretic, not on an easy ceremony of rehabilitation.
