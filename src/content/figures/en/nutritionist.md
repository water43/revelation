### Profile

The master nutritionist is a contemporary professional type in this gallery, not one exclusive biography. The work sits between nutrition science in labs and guidelines and the fridge, canteen, and takeout box: listen to complaints and history cues, read body composition, labs, and schedule, then turn protein, fat, carbohydrate, fiber, micronutrients, and water into **three meals a week that can still be eaten**. High level does not mean reciting RDAs; it means translating evidence into swappable dishes and sustainable portions, with a floor that survives social dinners, overtime, and travel.

Functionally the primary mark is Knowledge, with Service beside it: assessment, conversion, and risk recognition on one side; explanation, follow-up, and correction of panic dieting on the other. Attribute is Vaccine—stabilize the metabolic environment by norm and restoration, rather than rewriting the body with extremes for a short-term shape. Field is Market: clinics, gyms, corporate canteen advice, and remote follow-up in urban professional networks. Stage is Ultimate (Perfect): a completed form of the role, able to own complex cases, not merely forward “ten superfoods.”

### Extended notes

Unlike the crop farmer or fisher, the nutritionist rarely produces ingredients, yet decides how they enter the body’s ledger. Unlike the cook, the product is a **sustainable structure**, not a single peak of flavor. Unlike the physician—unless separately licensed—the core is not diagnosis and drugs but the design of eating behavior and fueling. Limits are plain: no menu outruns collapsed sleep and chronic stress; individual difference and nutrition misinformation arrive together; selling anxiety and supplement lists alone slides from Vaccine into marketing speech. In this gallery the role marks a modern division of labor: the land produces, the workshop processes and standardizes, and the master nutritionist in the market translates “what can be bought” into “how one can still eat and stay well.”
