### Profile

The master nutritionist is a contemporary professional type, not one exclusive biography. The work sits between nutrition evidence and the everyday table: listen to complaints and history cues, read body composition, labs, and schedule, then turn protein, fat, carbohydrate, fiber, micronutrients, and water into **three meals a week that can still be eaten**. High level does not mean reciting RDAs; it means translating evidence into swappable dishes and sustainable portions, with a floor that survives social dinners, overtime, and travel.

Functionally the primary mark is Knowledge, with Service beside it: assessment, conversion, and risk recognition on one side; explanation, follow-up, and correction of panic dieting on the other. Attribute is Vaccine—stabilize the metabolic environment by norm and restoration, rather than rewriting the body with extremes for a short-term shape. Field is Market: clinics, gyms, corporate canteen advice, and remote follow-up in urban professional networks. Stage is Ultimate (Perfect): a completed form of the role, able to own complex cases, not merely forward trending food lists.

### Extended notes

Day-to-day delivery usually includes individualized assessment, cyclic menus and swap lists, fueling shifts between training and rest days, and diet structures aligned with medical goals when chronic care is in play. Limits are plain: no menu alone outruns collapsed sleep and chronic stress; individual difference and nutrition misinformation arrive together; selling anxiety and supplement lists alone lets marketing speech replace professional judgment. What must be kept is the translation of “what can be bought” into “how one can still eat and stay with it.”
